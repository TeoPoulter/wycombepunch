# Details needed to complete the live service

The visual website and Punch Rush update are ready. These facts are needed to finish booking enquiries and the shared daily score:

1. **Business:** contracting operator/company name and public business contact address where appropriate.
2. **Contact:** public email, enquiry-receiving email, phone or WhatsApp number, and any confirmed Instagram handle.
3. **Hire offer:** whether the machine is available now, machine/model photos and setup requirements, hire durations, prices or quote-only pricing, delivery area/charges, supervision and participant suitability.
4. **Booking terms:** deposit/payment timing, cancellation/rescheduling and breakdown arrangements, plus agreed enquiry retention.
5. **Daily score:** anonymous shared score or nickname leaderboard; approval to access the chosen service account. The prepared implementation is an anonymous Cloudflare daily score with separate game-mode records and a UK-midnight reset.

Once supplied, update config and the actual visitor information together, connect the enquiry route, and verify receipt in the chosen inbox. GitHub Pages alone cannot receive form submissions or store a shared score.
